# Documented semantic changes in Indonesian (validation candidates)

Scope: cases whose change falls in 1950-2022. "Vis." = where the change should be visible: B = books 1950-2019, N = news 2017-2022.

## How to read this file

- A case is in the sourced tables only if I opened a page (or a downloaded PDF of it) that supports the sense change or the replacement. The "Source says" column paraphrases that page.
- Dates in the "When" column come from the source when I write "src". Otherwise they are my estimate and are marked "est." They are not documented by the cited page. Treat "est." dates as hypotheses.
- Many academic sources here are small student-style studies (one newspaper, one month, or a handful of tweets). They show that a sense exists and is labelled as a change, not when it started. Sense comparisons are usually against KBBI, not against a historical corpus.
- Source IDs (S1, S2, ...) are listed at the bottom.
- Pages I tried but could not read (HTTP 403, 404, or unreadable PDFs) are listed at the bottom. Nothing from them is used in the sourced tables.

## 2. COVID-19 news vocabulary (all N only)

All rows come from one study: S1, Avitaningrum and Savitri, Bapala 9(5), 2022, which analysed headlines from Jawa Pos in February 2021 and compared each sense with KBBI. The authors call 21 changes narrowing and attribute most to "pengembangan istilah" (an existing word given a new, Covid-related sense). Date for every row: the sense is visible in Feb 2021 (src); it started in 2020 (est.).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| klaster | group of things, gugus | origin area/group of Covid spread ("ancaman klaster baru") | narrowing | S1: "terjadi penyempitan makna yakni wilayah asal penyebaran virus Covid-19." |
| positif | sure, certain, definite | infected with Covid-19 ("rasio kasus positif") | new sense (listed under pengembangan istilah; S1 also calls it broadening in one place) | S1: "bermakna pasti, tegas, tentu, namun ... terpapar virus Covid-19." |

## Caveats for use as a validation set

- Strongest evidence of a datable change: oknum, diamankan, buruh, cina/tionghoa, cacat/disabilitas, daring/luring, penyintas, and the COVID senses (S1).
- Weak for the detector: all "proposed" glossary rows from S18, and section 6 (no dates).
- S1 treats a few words inconsistently (positif is called both broadening and narrowing; emas is glossed "obat"). Use its senses, not its type labels.
- The slang section is partly 2025-2026 social media data, so it may fall outside what the 2017-2022 news corpus contains.
