    Jumat

    How the word “jumat” changed the company it keeps over the years: its route on the chart of Kompas news, its company, and its numbers in each source.

    In 2011, Jumat was often on the same page as “sore”, “polisi”, and “tsunami”. By 2024, new beside it: Polres, “pelaku”, and “tersangka”. This name was on 16% of pages in 2024, about the same as 16% in 2011.

    In Kompas news, “jumat” appeared most often with “sore”, “wib”, and “polisi” in 2011; in 2024, with “pukul”, “wib”, and Polres.

    Its largest shift between two periods: 2.9.
