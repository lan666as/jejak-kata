    A year after leaving, a fifth is left

    A year out of office, SBY is down to 19% of his last year in Kompas, 6% in Tempo, 16% in Tribunnews. Of the 25 people who left seven top offices in 2012–2023, 23 have enough pages to count; their median is 16–25%. Those not forgotten: the ones who took another post, ran for office, or stood trial.

    19% SBY in 2015, of his last year in office, in Kompas

    Does the press follow power, or the person? There is a way to measure it: count how often a name is written in its last year in office, then look again a year later. How much is left?

    What this cannot tell you

    This is no charge against the press. Losing office is the most ordinary reason to be reported less.

    Election years make the drop look steeper. Year zero for SBY, Boediono and Hatta Rajasa is 2014, for Jusuf Kalla 2019: both election years, when every political name swells.

    One spelling of each name is counted (“sby”, not “susilo bambang yudhoyono”).

    The data ends in 2024. About what happened to Jokowi's name after October 2024, nothing can be said here.

    Who is counted. The second and third figures hold every person who left seven offices between 2012 and 2023: president, vice president, DPR speaker, governor of Jakarta, national police chief, armed forces commander and KPK chair. Acting holders are not counted, nor is Jokowi as governor, who left to become president. Year 0 is the year they left; for the two who left in January (Sutarman, Idham Azis), the year before. A dot exists only where the outlet has at least 80 pages with the name in year 0; that is why two armed forces commanders, Agus Suhartono and Moeldoko, are not drawn at all. The median left after a year: 16% in Kompas, 25% in Tempo, 25% in Tribunnews. Hatta Rajasa, who is in the first figure, held none of the seven offices: he was a coordinating minister and a vice-presidential candidate.
