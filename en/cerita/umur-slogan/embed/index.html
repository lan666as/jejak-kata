    What this cannot tell you

    A slogan leaving the news does not mean its programme stopped: the sea toll still sails, and the tax amnesty was repeated under another name.

    All that is measured is how often a phrase is written on one publisher's news pages, Kompas.

    Slogans still rising in 2024 have no ending to measure yet.

    Which slogans are counted. Nineteen names of central-government programmes and slogans that are among Kompas's 20 thousand most frequent words. The data starts in 2011, so of the SBY government only BLSM is caught; this is no comparison between presidents. Not in that vocabulary, and so absent: MP3EI, poros maritim, dana desa, saber pungli, Indonesia Emas, makan bergizi gratis. “indonesia maju” is also the name of a cabinet (2019) and of a coalition (2023).

    Four that are in the vocabulary but not drawn, and not all of them vanish: “program keluarga harapan” (a family welfare programme) holds at 0.05–0.21% since 2016, “blt” (cash transfers) returns to 0.22–0.39% in 2020–2022, “kampus merdeka” is at 0.08–0.13% since 2020, and “pengampunan pajak” follows “tax amnesty”. Routine aid programmes do not die the way slogans do.
