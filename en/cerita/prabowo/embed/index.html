    From candidate to defence minister, in one year

    Two defeats, then a seat in the cabinet. Within one year the word beside Prabowo's name went from “capres” to “menteri pertahanan”. The word “oposisi” is rare beside his name, but it is rare beside everyone's.

    4% → 38% “menteri pertahanan” beside his name, 2019 to 2020

    When did the news stop writing Prabowo as the rival? More suddenly than we remember. And one hunch about the word “oposisi” does not survive the comparison.

    What this cannot tell you

    What is measured is the words that share a page with a name: not the stance of the press, and not Prabowo himself.

    A new office brings new words; that is no finding.

    The rarity of “oposisi” is not particular to Prabowo. The word is indeed rare beside his name (1–3% of pages in 2015–2018, 5% at most, in 2019), but beside Megawati and PDI Perjuangan in 2012–2014, when they were out of government, the word is on 1–2% of pages. In 2019 it rose for everyone, highest beside Demokrat (12%) and PKS (11%).

    We got this wrong once. The first version of this story made the word's rarity its finding; this comparison shows that was wrong.

    A page can also be sharply critical without the word “oposisi”.

    The yearly figures are from one publisher, Kompas, and end in 2024: his first year as president is not here. In Tempo, by period, “oposisi” is 3.4% at most and “menteri pertahanan” goes from 1–2% to about 20%; in Tribunnews the phrase is not frequent enough to count.
