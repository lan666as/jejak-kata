    The KPK shrank from the news. The word “suspect” did not

    From 2012 to 2023 the word “kpk” shrank by more than half in three outlets. But in 2024 Tempo turned back, the verbs beside the KPK never softened, and there is as much news about suspects as ever.

    KPK shrank by more than half in three outlets, from 2012 to 2023

    Did the press go soft on corruption after the KPK law was revised in 2019? People have argued about it at length. The answer turns out to be neither yes nor no.

    What this cannot tell you

    This counts words, not cases. It cannot say whether corruption fell, whether enforcement fell, or whether editors chose to write about other things; all three draw the same chart.

    The ends of the lines are shaky. The pages collected per year are unequal: from Tribunnews only 5,514 for 2024 and 3,639 for 2011.

    One year is not yet a direction. Tempo's rise in 2024 is real in the data, but it is one year in one outlet.

    A phrase counts as a word of its own. One with a Wikipedia title of its own (“video viral”, “tindak pidana korupsi”) is not counted under the single word.

    “KPK” here is the abbreviation only, and “kejaksaan agung” the full phrase only, not “Kejagung”.

    The word “tersangka” counts every suspect, not only corruption suspects; in Kompas it moves up and down between 3.6% and 8.0%, and 2024 is its lowest.

    Other words beside the KPK did change: “pegawai” (staff) is on 26% of KPK pages in 2021, “penindakan” (enforcement) on 29% in 2023, “etik” (ethics) on 14% in 2024.
